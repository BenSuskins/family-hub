# Family Hub — App Store listing copy for version 1.3

- **App name:** Family Hub
- **Subtitle:** Family life, in sync
- **Promotional text:** See the day ahead, plan meals and share the small jobs that keep home running.

## Description

Family life has a lot of moving parts. Family Hub brings your household calendar, meals, chores, shopping and home supplies together in one shared app.

Start the day with a clear view of what is coming up. Plan meals for the week, keep family recipes close, share the chores and make a shopping list everyone can use.

### Keep everyone in the loop

- **Today:** See upcoming events, meal plans and household tasks at a glance.
- **Calendar:** Browse family events by day, week, month or year. Add the calendars your household follows.
- **Chores:** Assign jobs, set recurring schedules and keep track of what is done.
- **Meals and recipes:** Plan the week, save recipes and ingredients, and follow cooking steps in cook mode.
- **Shopping:** Share one list and add items as you notice they are running low.
- **Inventory:** Track household supplies by quantity or level.
- **Commute:** Check scheduled journeys between your chosen home and work stations.
- **Widgets and Apple Watch:** Keep useful plans nearby and continue cooking from your wrist.
- **Siri:** Use voice actions for common household tasks.

Family Hub connects to a Family Hub server provided by your household. Ask your household administrator for the server address and an account.
