Calendar & widgets

The Calendar you use today, in Warm Sage Cosier. A new widget set built around meals and the next event.

Original auditDesign notes and interactionsSelected Week view · compact all-dayCalendar boardWidget boardImage manifestValidation

Calendar: keep the familiar views

Year, Month, Day, and Week stay in place. Keep the zoom, swipe, Today, Back, and view-menu behaviour. Apply the warm canvas, sage selection, and soft surfaces.

Widgets: make the useful things clear

Use Today medium as the default. Put the next event beside dinner. Offer Tonight, Meal plan, Up next, and Chores as focused choices.

These are static proposals, not simulator captures or app changes. Calendar data, icon strokes, native controls, and system materials are illustrative. The clear and tinted widgets show design intent; WidgetKit controls their final appearance.
