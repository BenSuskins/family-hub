# Family Hub iOS — changes since version 1.2

This user-facing summary covers the iOS changes after the `iOS-1.2` tag (tagged 1 June 2026), through the changes on `main` included in the version 1.3 marketing set. It groups related changes and leaves out server maintenance and dependency updates.

## Today and planning

- Reworked Today into a clearer household overview with meals, upcoming events, chores and commute information.
- Added compact household widgets focused on the next event and meal.
- Improved loading and refresh behavior so existing household content stays visible while data updates or the connection is unavailable.

## Calendar

- Rebuilt the calendar with day, week, month and year views.
- Improved event navigation, all-day event presentation and accessibility.
- Added calendar subscriptions and administration tools for managing shared calendars.

## Meals and recipes

- Added a weekly meal planner and a shared family recipe book.
- Added recipe import from the iOS share sheet, grouped ingredients, cooking steps and cook mode.
- Added Apple Watch cooking handoff and improved recipe suggestions while planning meals.

## Chores and household

- Added recurring chore schedules, current-task management and household reminders.
- Added a shared shopping list and household inventory tracking by quantity or level.
- Improved the Food and Household overviews and tab navigation.

## Commute and family access

- Added configurable home and work stations with scheduled commute cards.
- Added family member invite sharing and expanded profile and household settings.

## Design and reliability

- Refreshed the iPhone app and widgets with the Warm Sage design, clearer typography and more consistent navigation.
- Improved text-size support, calendar accessibility, offline snapshots and image caching.

## Source range

The summary is based on the first-parent history after `iOS-1.2` through the `main` changes represented by PR #94. The screenshot pipeline in PR #95 supplies versioned marketing material and deterministic demo data; it does not add a release feature to the signed-in app.
